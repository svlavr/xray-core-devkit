# Explore and decide a design

Read `docs/PROJECT_SCOPE.md` and
`docs/WORKFLOW.md#invention-criticism-and-experiments`. Main supplies the original
outcome, authority, actual consumer and unresolved decision. Keep a clear direct
change direct; this skill adds no mandatory design document or team roster.

Main uses `xray-orchestration` to dispatch useful ready independent questions.
An owner explorer maps reachable native behavior; `mu_solution_researcher` and
`mu_upstream_researcher` resolve actual source/API/version/license facts;
`mu_innovator` proposes complete alternatives. Preserve first proposals before
exchanging critique. Consider native/configuration and small direct approaches
as well as useful unfamiliar mechanisms. No forced novelty or idea quota.

Choose a problem-solving technique only when it helps: causal analogy transfers
a mechanism with its limits; a contradiction identifies competing requirements;
morphology varies actual design dimensions; assumption reversal and first
principles test an unsupported premise. These are lenses, not framework imports,
business personas, random-method registries or evidence of correctness.

Give `mu_design_critic` the original task, candidates and their evidence. Seek a
falsifying case, incompatible owners, missing consumer, unjustified constraint
or stronger complete alternative. Supported acceptance with zero findings is
valid. Researchers may disprove the critic as well as the creator. Resolve facts
from reachable source; do not decide by agreement or a count of votes.

`mu_test_architect` defines a deciding observation. Use
`xray-decision-experiment` to assign the existing `mu_go_worker` an explicit
experimenter mode and exact scratch write set. Main returns actual results and
critic findings to the innovator, preserves revisions and chooses on evidence.
An experiment recommendation, executed fixture or design report does not close
the requested capability; continue through `xray-feature-delivery` and its real
consumer acceptance.

For competing explanations, assign independent hypotheses with confirming and
falsifying observations and common meaningful acceptance. Keep coupled state or
experiments under one owner. For algorithm/workflow improvement, use
`xray-agent-evaluation` to validate the evaluator, retain successes/failures and
select on stable observations rather than spreading a noisy single-run winner.

Requirements are testable assumptions. Explain material evidence-based revisions
and resolve genuinely human product/authority choices; never silently omit an
agreed outcome. Main retains the selected parent model and integration/Git
authority. Advisory roles use Sol 6.1 high; role count and capacity 23 impose no
fixed team size, round count or quota. Workers do not dispatch nested agents.
